# Topic Lottery Subject Collection

1,000 subjects across 10 categories, with 100 subjects in each category. Draw a subject, research it independently, choose your angle, and record a one-minute explanation. Broad subjects can be narrowed to one idea or example for the video.

These titles are starting points for research. For psychology, distinguish supported findings from popular claims; for religion and spirituality, distinguish evidence, interpretation, and belief.
